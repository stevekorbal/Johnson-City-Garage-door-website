## Key Factors That Impact Your Repair Price

1. **Spring Type & Size:** Heavy wood or insulated carriage doors require thick gauge, high-torque torsion springs, which carry higher manufacturing costs than basic extension springs.
2. **Standard vs. High-Cycle Springs:** Standard springs last approximately 10,000 cycles (roughly 7-10 years of average daily use). High-cycle springs offer up to 25,000+ cycles for a slight premium.
3. **Labor & Emergency Service:** Standard daytime service calls carry fixed rates. If you need 24/7 emergency dispatch on weekends or late nights, an emergency service fee may apply.
4. **Safety Cable & Bearing Replacement:** If the spring failure caused your end bearings or safety cables to snap, replacing those auxiliary parts adds $30 – $75 to the job.

---

## Why DIY Spring Repair Is Hazardous

Garage door springs operate under **tremendous torque and mechanical tension** equal to the full weight of your door (150 to 350+ lbs). Attempting to adjust or unbolt torsion springs with improper winding bars or household tools can result in severe physical injury or property damage.

Professional technicians use calibrated winding bars, winding cones, and vice grips designed specifically to balance torsion shafts safely.

## Signs Your Garage Door Spring Is Broken

- **A loud gunshot-like pop** echoed from the garage.
- The door opens **2 to 4 inches** and stops, triggering the opener's safety reverse.
- You can visibly see a **gap or break** in the wound steel spring above the door header.
- The top section of the door appears bent or jammed when the opener pulls.
- The garage door feels extremely heavy when trying to lift it manually.
